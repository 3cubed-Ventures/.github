### What we do

3cubed Ventures is a seed and early-stage venture capital firm. Investing is the
primary business — backing founders early and staying close as they build.

### What lives here

This organization is the home of 3cubed's **exploratory software projects**:
tools and experiments we build for our own use, alongside the systems that run
the firm. Most of the work here is private.
